# RAICE LABZ — Audio Guide Scripts (English)

**Voice:** Male, warm, clear, instructional — like a knowledgeable colleague guiding a mill operator.
**Pace:** Normal conversational speed, brief pause between sentences.
**File naming for recorded clips:** `{clip_id}_en.mp3` (e.g. `procurement_preparation_en.mp3`)
**Covers:** Procurement Analysis, Production Analysis, Milled Rice Quality Analysis — each with 3 clips: Test Preparation, Live Analysis, Insights & Reports.

---

## Procurement Analysis

### 1. Test Preparation — `procurement_preparation`

Welcome to Procurement Analysis. Use this before you buy paddy from a farmer or trader, so you can check its quality and agree on a fair price. Start by confirming your mill details — operator name, mill name, and location. Then fill in the grain information: variety, process, harvest season, and month. Choose whether to measure your sample by weight or by grain count, and set how many samples to test — testing more samples gives a more reliable result. If you like, enter the purchase details too — vehicle number, mandi name, price per kilogram, and bags — so the app can work out your expected yield and margin. Once everything is filled in, tap Continue to start Live Analysis.

### 2. Live Analysis — `procurement_live`

Live Analysis has started. Place your paddy sample on the machine and let the camera scan and sort every grain. Please don't remove the sample or refresh the page while scanning is running, or the analysis will stop and you'll need to start over. If you set more than one sample, repeat this for each one. Once all samples are done, the app will take you straight to Insights and Reports.

### 3. Insights & Reports — `procurement_reports`

Here are your results. Head Rice percentage shows how much good, whole rice this paddy will yield — higher is better for you. Broken Rice, Chalky, Discoloured, Immature, and Foreign Matter are quality deductions — the higher these numbers, the lower the paddy is worth. The app also uses these results to estimate your husk, bran, and head rice yield in kilograms, and your overall cost and margin. If the quality is good, go ahead and confirm the purchase. If Chalky, Discoloured, or Broken numbers are high, use them to negotiate a lower price, or reject the lot. Tap Download Report to save a PDF copy for your records.

## Production Analysis

### 1. Test Preparation — `production_preparation`

Welcome to Production Analysis. This checks rice quality directly on your milling line while it's running. First, choose a single machine, or a full series — a series tests every machine on the line one by one, like the sortex, grader, and colour sorter, so you can see exactly where quality drops. Select your machine or series, fill in the grain information — variety, process, harvest season, and month — then set your sample size and number of samples. Tap Continue to start Live Analysis.

### 2. Live Analysis — `production_live`

Live Analysis has started for this machine. Let the camera finish scanning each sample before touching the machine or the app. If you're running a full series, once this machine's samples are done, you'll be prompted to move to the next machine — follow the same steps there. Don't refresh the page or leave partway through, as it will interrupt the run for the whole series.

### 3. Insights & Reports — `production_reports`

Your production results are ready. For a single machine, you'll see Head Rice, Broken Rice, Chalky, Discoloured, and Whiteness Index directly. For a series, switch between machines to compare quality at each stage of the line — if Chalky or Broken rice jumps sharply after one machine, that machine may need cleaning, a settings check, or maintenance. Use these numbers to fine-tune your machines and improve head rice yield. Tap Download Report to save the full report as a PDF.

## Milled Rice Quality Analysis

### 1. Test Preparation — `milled_preparation`

Welcome to Milled Rice Quality Analysis — your final quality check on finished rice before packaging or sale. Your region, Basmati or Non-Basmati, is already set from Settings and shown at the top. Fill in the variety, process, harvest season, and month, then set your sample size and number of samples. Tap Continue to start Live Analysis.

### 2. Live Analysis — `milled_live`

Live Analysis is now running on your milled rice sample. Let the camera finish scanning and classifying every grain before moving on. Once all samples are complete, you'll move automatically to the Reports step.

### 3. Insights & Reports — `milled_reports`

Here is your final quality report. For Non-Basmati rice, check Head Rice, Broken Rice, Chalky, and Discoloured percentages, along with the Whiteness Index grade — Super White, White, Cream, or Golden. For Basmati rice, you'll also see Translucency and length categories like Second One, Tibar, Dubar, and Mongra. These numbers tell you the grade this batch is fit for — high Head Rice with a bright Whiteness grade means premium quality, good for export or top-tier sale. Higher Broken, Chalky, or Discoloured percentages mean the batch is better sold at a lower grade, or blended. Tap Download Report to save this as a PDF for your records or your buyer.
