Figure · Grain-level measurement

Every value produced per grain, then aggregated

Each grain is detected and outlined. The projected chalk region is measured as a proportion of the grain's projected area. Length and width are measured directly; aspect ratio is computed from them — which is why a TMA percentage carries more weight than a percentage taken from a scoop.

1. Detected grain — every grain in the field of view is detected and outlined.

2. Projected chalky area — the chalk region is measured as a proportion of the grain's projected area.

3–4. Length & width — measured directly; aspect ratio is computed from them.
