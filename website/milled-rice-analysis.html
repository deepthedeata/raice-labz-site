Milled Rice Analysis

The final quality gate — verify the finished, milled rice before it's bagged and dispatched.

Why it matters

Confirm what leaves the mill matches what you promised

The finished product is what customers actually receive. Milled Rice Analysis gives every outgoing batch an objective, repeatable quality check — grading, grain shape and whiteness — so what ships is consistent, batch after batch.

  • ✓ Classify Basmati and Non-Basmati rice with dedicated grading criteria for each
  • ✓ Catch inconsistent batches before they leave the mill
  • ✓ Keep a full record of finished-product quality for every batch, every day
What it measures

A complete finished-product profile

Basmati / Non-Basmati Grading

Category-specific classification, with quality indicators tuned to each rice type.

Head Rice & Brokens

The finished-grain equivalent of the procurement breakdown — accepted, rejected, foreign matter.

Grain Dimensions

Length, width and aspect ratio, measured directly from captured grain images.

Whiteness

A visual whiteness read on the finished grain, tracked sample to sample.

Variety-wise Comparison

Compare finished quality across every variety passing through the mill.

Every Sample, Reported

Nothing is summarised away — every individual sample is available in full detail in Reports & Analytics.

The last gate before dispatch

Blend & Pack Analysis

Right before packing, TMA verifies the final blended product one more time — confirming composition and broken classification so what's about to be bagged and sealed matches the specification you sold.

  • ✓ Verify blended composition matches the target specification
  • ✓ Confirm broken classification is within agreed limits
  • ✓ Catch a mismatched batch before it's sealed and shipped
Sample-to-sample, day after day

Track finished quality as it happens

Milled Rice Analysis is built around the same sample-to-sample view used across TMA — every individual sample plotted in order, across every day in range, with the best sample, the worst sample, and any statistically abnormal sample called out automatically.
