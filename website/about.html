About TMA

Grain quality intelligence, built for real mills

TMA — Total Mill Analyzer — is a product by APIT, built to give rice mills objective, sample-level visibility into grain quality at every stage of the process.

Our approach

Measure quality where decisions actually happen

Grain quality is usually judged by eye — at the gate when paddy arrives, on the floor as it moves through the mill, and at the end when rice is bagged for dispatch. TMA replaces that guesswork with grain-by-grain imaging and classification, so the same objective standard applies at every stage: Procurement, Production and Milled Rice.

Every sample is guided step by step, with voice narration available in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam — so any operator on the mill floor can run a full analysis. Results flow straight into one connected Reports & Analytics history, so quality trends are visible over time, not just on the day a sample was taken.
