Reports & Analytics

Every sample from Procurement, Production and Milled Rice Analysis lands in one filterable, analysable history.

Two views, one dataset

Reports view, and Analytics view

Filter by date range, report type, variety, season and machine to pull up individual sample records and full trial detail — or flip to the Analytics view to see the same filtered data as trend lines, comparisons and statistics.

  • ✓ Reports — the individual, detailed record for every sample and trial
  • ✓ Analytics — the same filtered data, visualised as trends and comparisons
  • ✓ One domain analysed at a time — Procurement, Production, or Milled Rice — so every chart stays focused
Inside the Analytics view

Built for sample-to-sample detail

Every chart is driven by individual samples, not just daily averages — with a dedicated chart per parameter so different units and scales never get muddled together.

Sample-to-Sample Trend Charts

Every sample plotted in order, labelled by sample number and date, one chart per parameter.

Control Charts

Mean and upper/lower control limits overlaid on the trend, so drift and instability are visible immediately.

Best, Worst & Abnormal Samples

The strongest sample, the weakest sample, and any statistical outliers are surfaced automatically.

Machine-to-Machine Comparison

Grouped comparisons across every machine, plus a machine × day quality heatmap.

Series-to-Series Comparison

Compare full multi-machine production runs against one another, not just single machines.

Variety Breakdowns

Composition and radar views showing how quality differs from one paddy or rice variety to the next.
